# Report a content correction

Use the **Content correction** form with the exact location, expected behavior, and the edition or publisher that lets a reviewer verify it. A precise reference is sufficient to open a report. For an edition-specific page error, distinguish the canonical surah and ayah from the page and line in that edition. For a poem or a curriculum rule, give the line or rule identifier and explain the intended change. [Form fields](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/content.yml)

For example, a report can say “The planner selects the first two lines of my six-line practice poem. After I mark them complete, the next session still selects line two.” Include a synthetic reproduction and the expected range. Contributors can verify this behavior without copying the poem itself.

If you propose adding content, name its rights holder, version, source, exact terms, attribution and permitted uses. Confirm rights for public raw-file and binary redistribution, including any generated copies. For scripture and religious commentary, identify the edition and ask for qualified editorial review. Keep private permission correspondence out of issues. The public tree deliberately uses synthetic examples without third-party religious corpora.
