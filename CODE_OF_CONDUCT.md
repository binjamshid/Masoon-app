# Community conduct

Treat learners, contributors and reviewers with respect. Masoon works with Arabic religious texts and learning traditions. When editions or readings differ, identify the source, state the difference precisely, and leave space for a qualified reviewer to check it. Review the proposed change on its merits.

Harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory behavior, and disclosure of private information are grounds for moderation. These expectations apply to project issues, reviews and project-related communications. Report conduct concerns privately to **support@binjamshid.com**. Give the maintainer enough context to locate the incident and avoid including unrelated personal data.

The maintainer may clarify expectations, edit or remove inappropriate public material, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation. If context is missing or a decision needs reconsideration, reply privately with the relevant facts.
