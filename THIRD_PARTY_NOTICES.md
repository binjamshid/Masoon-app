## Civil-date arithmetic

The Swift, Kotlin and TypeScript planner engines use civil-date formulas documented by Howard Hinnant. Hinnant dedicated the formulas to the public domain in the summary of his original article. [Chrono-Compatible Low-Level Date Algorithms](https://howardhinnant.github.io/date_algorithms.html#Summary)
